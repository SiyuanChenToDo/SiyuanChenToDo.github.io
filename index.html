About

WHO I AM

I'm Siyuan Chen (陈思远), an undergraduate student majoring in Artificial Intelligence at Huazhong Agricultural University (HZAU) — a 211 & Double First-Class university in Wuhan, China.

My research sits at the intersection of LLM-based multi-agent systems and automated scientific discovery. I lead FIG-MAC, a framework in which a fine-grained inspiration graph orchestrates how agents collaborate, retrieve, and iteratively reason — with the goal of generating scientific hypotheses that are not just plausible, but genuinely novel.

Beyond hypothesis generation, I work on knowledge graph-enhanced reasoning & retrieval and multimodal understanding — most recently, teaching models to read comics the way humans do: panel by panel, in narrative order.

I'm currently seeking 2027 Fall PhD / direct-PhD opportunities, as well as research collaborations and visiting internships in LLM agents, AI4Science, and reasoning.

Research

WHAT I THINK ABOUT
R·1

LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems

How should a society of language-model agents be structured so that debate, division of labor, and iterative critique compound into discoveries none of them could reach alone?

R·2

Automated Scientific Discovery

Modeling the "spark" of research as a fine-grained inspiration graph — linking papers, concepts, and methods — so hypothesis generation becomes a navigable, structured process rather than a lucky guess.

R·3

Knowledge Graphs & Retrieval-Augmented Reasoning

Grounding LLM reasoning in structured knowledge: graph-enhanced retrieval, link prediction, and reasoning paths that stay faithful to evidence.

R·4

Multimodal Understanding

Structured reasoning over images and text together — from comic narrative order to scientific figures — where layout and sequence carry the meaning.
